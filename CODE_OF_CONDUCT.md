# Code of Conduct

This learning community is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Participants should use welcoming language, respect different levels of experience, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe learning environment.
